## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating technical fit for full-time engineering roles. They visit to assess qualifications, experience depth, technical capabilities, and cultural alignment before making interview/hire decisions.

## Product Purpose

A professional portfolio and resume gateway that positions Sravana S Parimi as a Senior Software Engineer III with documented project contributions. Success means converting qualified visitors into interview requests and offers.

## Positioning

Combines three differentiating mechanisms:

1. **Architect studio design** with cobalt, cool paper, ink, pale lime, Space Grotesk typography, and three user-approved compositions selectable through a sliding control.
2. **Auth-protected resume access** that creates a trust barrier and deeper vetting context — employers must authenticate before accessing sensitive career information.
3. **Concrete project contributions** that explain problems, engineering work, and outcomes without unsupported numerical claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, recruiter searches, referrals, or direct domain entry. Expected workflows:

- Browse home to assess expertise and services at a glance.
- Review work/portfolio for shipped project examples and technical depth.
- View about page for background and career narrative.
- Authenticate and access gated resume for formal vetting.
- Contact via email form for collaboration inquiries.

## Product Principles

1. **Evidence over assertion** — claims supported by career facts or shipped examples; numerical outcomes require evidence.
2. **Craft as positioning** — premium visual design and interaction signal technical standards.
3. **Trust through friction** — authentication and gating create credibility; deeper access signals serious intent from employers.
4. **Technical clarity** — stack (React, Next.js, TypeScript, Java, Spring Boot) and specializations (architecture, performance, leadership) are explicit and findable.
